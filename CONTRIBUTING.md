# Contributing

Contributions must preserve the "does nothing" principle and keep the experience quiet and complete.

## Allowed

- Micro-interactions that remain subtle and calm
- Ambient visual modes that do not add controls or complexity
- Performance and energy efficiency improvements
- Philosophical or documentation edits

## Not allowed

- Anything that introduces goals, tracking, or optimization
- Onboarding, accounts, sync, analytics, or notifications
- Feature growth that changes the core premise
